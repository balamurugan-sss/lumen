# SNOW WHITE — Treatment (80s Cel Anime)

Brief (Boss, 2026-10-21): full Grimm storyline, 80s Cel Anime style,
English narration, Vietnamese subtitles, duration follows the story
(~3.5–4 min). No storyboard review; all decisions delegated to Mea.
Source text: the Brothers Grimm tale (public domain). Disney-specific
inventions are excluded: the dwarfs get no names and no signature
designs; the huntsman brings a boar's heart; the ending softens the
red-hot shoes into flight and a cracked mirror (decided in the brief).

## Three candidate structures

**A — Narrated storybook (CHOSEN).** One English narrator carries the
whole tale over painted plates and held cels; characters act in
pantomime with cycles and a few lip-flap inserts. Every story beat
gets its own scene grammar from the style's camera table. Strength:
the full arc fits 3.5–4 min, the narration IS the storyline Boss
asked for, and held drawings + cycles are exactly what the 80s
limited-animation grammar does best. Cost: character acting must read
without dialogue.

**B — Mock anime opening + episode fragments.** 60 s of chrome-title
character roster, then three "episode" fragments. Rejected: it
fragments the storyline into highlights; Boss asked for the story
itself, not a trailer for it.

**C — Dialogue two-hander.** Snow White and the dwarfs carry scenes
in dubbed dialogue with lip sync. Rejected: dialogue coverage of 13
beats doubles the script and the head/lip workload, pushes duration
past 5 min, and an English-dub comedy tone fights the tale's mood.

## Structure A — scenes (chronological, narration-led)

1. **Power-on + title.** Tape power-on (dot → line → picture), chrome
   italic title SNOW WHITE with a katakana line, star glint, brass hit.
2. **The wish.** Winter window plate; the first queen sews, pricks her
   finger; three drops of blood on snow in an ebony frame. Snowfall
   cycle. (Held still, slow push.)
3. **The mirror.** Night chamber, backlit mirror glow; the queen
   asks; the mirror's face answers — this time naming Snow White.
   ECU on the queen's eyes (resolve). 
4. **The huntsman.** Forest plate; he raises the knife behind her,
   cannot strike; insert: a boar's heart in the box. She runs —
   side-tracking parallax, mild speed grammar.
5. **The cottage.** Dusk forest → tiny house; insert of seven little
   plates, cups, beds; she sleeps across the seventh bed.
6. **The dwarfs return.** Seven backlit silhouettes in the doorway;
   wonder, then the morning pact two-shot (lip-flap inserts on the
   eldest's warning: let no one in). They march to the mine at dawn.
7. **Three visits (montage).** Mirror insert re-checks; the queen in
   disguise sells laces (doorstep two-shot; Snow White falls; dwarfs
   burst in and cut the laces), then the poisoned comb (same rhythm,
   faster), each visit separated by a tracking-noise tape beat.
8. **The apple.** Insert with bokeh and sliding highlight: half red,
   half white. The queen eats the white half; ECU Snow White's eyes;
   the bite — impact frames — she falls. One beat of true silence.
9. **The glass coffin.** The film's harmony still: coffin on the hill
   under blossom, slow pan; the dwarfs keep watch; seasons pass in a
   colour-model change on the same composition.
10. **The prince.** Backlit arrival; the plea; servants shoulder the
    coffin, stumble on a root — insert: the piece of apple falls from
    her lips; eye-opening in three drawings (ECU); she sits up.
11. **The wedding + the mirror breaks.** Bright wide of the feast;
    far away, the queen smashes... no — she flees as the mirror
    cracks on its own insert (radial crack lines), her coach gone
    into the night. 
12. **Ending.** The cottage composition repainted at golden hour,
    everyone at the door; slow crane up to a starry sky; chrome
    THE END flickers on; tape power-off dot.

## Characters and colour models

One head function per §2 of the style (long egg face, almond eyes,
angel-ring hair), drawn on model sheets first and approved against
the style before production frames.

- **Snow White** — accent colour: ribbon red (the one pop in every
  lighting set). Sets: castle day, forest dusk, cottage amber,
  golden-hour. Black hair, ebony-dark line colour.
- **The Queen** — violet and gold; chamber-night set with cyan mirror
  rim; disguise set (dull brown pedlar) for the visits.
- **The Mirror** — a face in cyan backlit glow on the emissive plate.
- **The Huntsman, the Prince** — simple two-tone models; the prince
  backlit on arrival.
- **Seven dwarfs** — one shared body/head model, seven palette, hat
  and beard-shape variations; distinguished by colour and prop only,
  never named (Grimm leaves them unnamed; names are a Disney layer).

Colour models per place/time, never a filter: winter noon (pale
cobalt, white), chamber night (indigo, violet, cyan, gold), forest
dusk (deep green, amber windows), cottage interior (amber, wood
brown, chalk green), mine morning (cobalt, sand), apple noon
(cobalt, white cumulus, apple red), coffin hill (golden hour),
wedding day (bright cobalt and gold), star field (indigo).

## Motion and grammar

Characters on twos (12 fps), beats on threes (8 fps); camera, snow,
flares and light sweeps on ones. Cycles: hair ribbons (4-drawing sine
cycle), snowfall, blossom fall, mirror shimmer, marching legs.
Native moves used: small flat hero in a huge painted world (forest,
coffin hill), stillness as drama (coffin harmony still, mirror ECU),
backlit light (mirror, doorway silhouettes, prince), insert with
bokeh (heart box, apple, apple piece), colour-model change (seasons
over the coffin composition), tape artefacts as punctuation
(power-on, tracking noise between the queen's visits, power-off).

## Sound

Original score in the style's palette, storybook register: FM
electric piano and FM bells (music-box motif for cottage and coffin),
string pad, soft synth-brass hits on the title and the bite, slap
bass and gated snare kept out of the tale's quiet scenes and used
only as a light city-pop lilt under the dwarfs' march and the
wedding. Voice: one English narrator (Kokoro), short lines, verified
line by line with whisper ASR. Mix per style: voice ~10 dB over
ducked music, two-pass loudnorm to −14 LUFS.

## Subtitles

Vietnamese, translated from the narration lines, composited inside
the CRT pass per the style (Barlow Semi Condensed 600, cream-yellow,
thick dark outline, ~96 px above the bottom), each held ≥ speech +
0.6 s; the same cue table is exported to `snow-white.srt`.

## Format

1920×1080 (16:9 theatrical), 24 fps, duration set by the narration
(target 3:30–4:00). Fonts self-hosted in the project. CREDITS lists
the Grimm source (public domain), fonts (OFL) and the library (tool
only). No library credit or watermark in the film.
